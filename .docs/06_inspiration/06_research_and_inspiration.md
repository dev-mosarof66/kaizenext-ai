# 06 — Research & Inspiration

We researched the leading AI / engineering / product company websites to anchor the bar. The developer should bookmark and study every one of these. Below: what to copy from each, what to skip.

---

## A. The "north star" five — copy the most from these

### 1. **Linear** — `https://linear.app`

- **Copy:** the restrained motion, the dark-mode product screenshots inside floating panels, the eyebrow-label section system (`Intake → Plan → Build → Diffs → Monitor`), the way every section has **one product screenshot and one paragraph**, no clutter.
- **Skip:** Linear is monochrome / blue-purple. Our brand is green/orange — but the layout discipline is what to take.

### 2. **Vercel** — `https://vercel.com`

- **Copy:** the dual-CTA hero (`Start Deploying` + `Get a Demo`), the gradient mesh hero background (we use green/orange instead of black), the product showcase organized into pillars, the dark/light theme toggle in footer, the OG image system.
- **Skip:** their dense feature density on subpages — we want more whitespace.

### 3. **Anthropic** — `https://anthropic.com`

- **Copy:** the editorial card grid for "Latest Releases" (we'll reuse for Insights), the typography hierarchy (large display + serif accents on key words — they do it the same way), the generous footer with every link organized, the calm pacing.
- **Skip:** the very text-heavy hero — we want a more visual hero.

### 4. **Cresta** — `https://cresta.com`

- **Copy:** how they show **enterprise logos repeatedly** (different cuts of the same set throughout the page builds trust), how they use a **rotating headline** in the hero (Transform / Automate / Analyze / Augment) — we can do "Workflows / Voice / Vision / Custom", how they **embed a background video in the hero** to make it feel alive, how they pack **testimonials with metrics** (58% containment rate, 20% revenue increase).
- **Skip:** the slightly enterprise-y feel — Kaizenext is more product-y.

### 5. **ElevenLabs** — `https://elevenlabs.io`

- **Copy:** the **three-pillar product structure** (Creative / Agents / API → for us, Solutions / Product / Custom), the **API code snippet block** that shows a curl/JS sample (we should add this on the Custom AI page to show technical depth), the **research timeline** (we'll have a "From the lab" timeline showing past project highlights), the **audio sample play buttons** (we'll do the same with voice samples on the Voice AI page).
- **Skip:** the very dev-tools-feel — we serve non-dev SMB buyers too.

## B. Specific patterns to lift from each

### **Glean** — `https://glean.com`
- The **integration logo wall** (100+ logos in a grid) — perfect template for our "Stack we work with" sections on solution pages.
- **Use-case row** (Engineering / Support / Sales / People / IT) with a screenshot per role.

### **Tribe AI** — `https://tribe.ai`
- The **dark-bg + glass-morphism + partnership cards** for "Deep Partnerships with the Frontier" → we mirror with our "Technical partner of [Saudi football product]" + "AI partnerships with OpenAI / Anthropic / ElevenLabs".
- **"Featured by" press logos** strip (Bloomberg, Forbes, Nasdaq, TechCrunch).

### **Retool** — `https://retool.com`
- **Four-pillar value prop strip** (Build / Launch / Scale / Govern). We use a four-pillar version: **Discover / Design / Build / Operate**.
- **"$8M saved · 10x reduction in dev time" stat ribbon** — copy this into our case studies.

### **VEED** — `https://veed.io`
- The **G2-badge cluster** with star ratings + "1000+ reviews" near the hero — we replicate once we have reviews. For now, swap with **"AU + UAE + KSA clients"** trust nugget.
- **Bento grid product showcase** (Generate / Subtitles / Brand) — perfect template for showing 3 product capabilities at a glance.

### **Veo (sports cameras)** — `https://veo.com`
- Most relevant for our **Computer Vision / football case study page**.
- **Copy:** how they show **broadcast footage with overlay** in the hero, the **sport-specific switcher** (football / rugby / hockey) — we can use this to show our vision capabilities apply beyond football.
- **Customer logo carousel** with real club crests — adds enormous credibility.

### **Cal.com** — `https://cal.com` (also our booking provider)
- The **embedded scheduler** UX is our reference for the `/contact` page.
- The way they have a clean dark hero + bright orange accents — directly aligned with our brand (green vs black, but otherwise identical).

### **Stripe** — `https://stripe.com`
- The **animated workflow diagram** with little dots flowing along connecting lines — we replicate on the Workflow Automation page.

### **Persistent Systems** — `https://persistent.com`
- More **enterprise-services oriented** than we are — but their **services + industries grid** is exactly what we want for Solutions hub. They organize by: Cloud, Data, Consulting, Engineering, CX, Automation, Security. We do the equivalent: Workflow / Ad Automation / Voice / Vision / Custom.

## C. What NOT to copy (anti-patterns we've seen)

These show up on lower-quality AI agency sites — avoid all of them:

1. **Generic "abstract gradient swoosh" hero with no product**. Show real product UI.
2. **Stock photos of diverse-people-pointing-at-laptops**. Real client work or nothing.
3. **"As featured in"** with logos of websites that aren't actually featuring you.
4. **Vague service buckets** like "Strategy", "Innovation", "Digital Transformation". Ours are concrete: Workflow / Ad Automation / Voice / Vision / Custom.
5. **Chat widget bot in bottom-right** asking "Hi, can we help?". On-brand chat is fine but the default Intercom/Drift bot is anti-luxury.
6. **Long forms on the contact page**. Cal.com embed + 4-field fallback only.
7. **Auto-playing audio anywhere**. The voice widget activates on user click only.
8. **Carousel that doesn't pause on hover.** Cresta does this and it's mildly annoying — we will pause.
9. **Pricing hidden behind "Contact us"** for everything. The Ad Automation product has public pricing tiers ($149 / $449 / $1,290).

## F. Final founder note for the developer

The bar here is **Linear / Vercel / Anthropic**, not "good for an agency in Bangladesh". If you're not sure whether something looks good enough, take a screenshot, put it next to a Linear screenshot, and ask yourself if a senior PM at a US tech company would be impressed.

When in doubt: **fewer elements, more whitespace, sharper typography, real product UI, restrained motion**.
